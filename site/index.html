What it does能做什麼

A workspace with rules you can inspect.

規則看得見的工作空間。

01

Group permissions

群組權限

Assign providers and models by group. Owners, administrators, compliance reviewers and members have different capabilities.

依群組分配供應商與模型。擁有者、管理員、法遵審核者與成員各有不同權限。

02

Content checks

內容檢查

Inspect prompts, extracted uploads and responses for sensitive content. Apply configured actions from flagging and redaction to blocking or approval.

檢查提示詞、上傳檔案擷取文字與回覆中的敏感內容，依設定標記、遮蔽、阻擋或要求核准。

03

Budget control

預算控制

Reserve spend before a provider request and settle actual usage afterward, including when the browser closes.

在請求供應商之前保留費用，之後依實際用量結算，瀏覽器中途關閉也會處理。

04

Audit exports

稽核匯出

Keep append-only, hash-chained audit records and export CSV, JSON or NDJSON for review.

保留只能附加、以雜湊鏈串接的稽核紀錄，並匯出 CSV、JSON 或 NDJSON 供檢視。

05

Retention and holds

資料保留與保存

Apply workspace retention windows and scoped legal holds. Overdue content is removed in bounded batches.

套用工作空間的保留期限與指定範圍的法律保存，過期內容以有上限的批次清除。

06

Files and models

檔案與模型

Extract text from common office files and PDFs. Connect configured APIs from seven built-in providers or a validated HTTPS endpoint.

從常見 Office 檔案與 PDF 擷取文字，連接七家內建供應商的已設定 API，或通過驗證的 HTTPS 端點。

Works today

目前可用

  • Local accounts, optional TOTP, group model access and account recovery.
  • 本機帳號、選用 TOTP、群組模型權限與帳號復原。
  • Content checks, budget settlement, audit exports and retention controls are described in the public source and README.
  • 公開原始碼與 README 記錄內容檢查、預算結算、稽核匯出與保留控制。

Limits and not yet

限制與尚未完成

  • Selected features send data to configured providers and other documented external services; self-hosting does not make those calls local.
  • 選用功能會把資料送到已設定的供應商及文件列出的外部服務；自行架設不會讓這些呼叫變成本機處理。
  • The audit chain has no external anchor and cannot prove integrity against an attacker who rewrites the entire database.
  • 稽核鏈沒有外部錨點，不能對抗改寫整份資料庫並重算鏈結的攻擊者。
  • Deployments need their own capacity, backup and operational acceptance. Review the project privacy and security documents before enabling external features.
  • 部署環境需要自己的容量、備份與維運驗收。開啟對外功能前，先閱讀專案的隱私與安全文件。
